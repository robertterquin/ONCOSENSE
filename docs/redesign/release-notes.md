# Release Notes — Redesign

Draft for the first release of the redesigned app (redesign Phase 7). Check the wording against the build before submitting.

## Short version (Play Store "What's new", under 500 characters)

> OncoSense has a new look, made to be calmer and easier to read. Five tabs: Today, Learn, Journey, Community and Help. Guided self-checks remind you when one is due and can be read aloud. Journey is now optional and adds appointments and a summary you can share with your doctor. Larger text options, dark mode and a crisis hotline one tap away on Help. We removed the news feed and wellness reminders to keep the app focused.

## Full version (App Store, TestFlight and Play internal testing)

### What's new

- **A new, calmer look.** Lavender for all cancers instead of pink, and an easier-to-read font (Atkinson Hyperlegible) built for people with low vision.
- **Five tabs:**
  - **Today** shows your next step: a self-check that's due, your check-in or your next appointment.
  - **Learn** puts self-check guides, cancer types and ways to lower your risk in one place, with search.
  - **Journey** is for people going through treatment, finished with treatment or caring for someone.
  - **Community** is where you ask questions and hear from people who've been there.
  - **Help** has hotlines, screening centers and support groups, filtered by the region you pick.
- **Guided self-checks.**
  - Go step by step, or tap the speaker to have each step read aloud.
  - We'll remind you when your next check is due, and you can choose which guides count.
- **Journey, now optional.**
  - Tell us what brings you here and the app shows what matters to you.
  - Add appointments and get a reminder the evening before.
  - Share a short PDF summary with your doctor. Nothing is shared until you choose where.
- **Reminders you control.** Choose when each reminder comes. There's also an optional weekly health tip.
- **Easier to use:**
  - Three text sizes, plus support for your phone's own larger text.
  - Dark mode that follows your phone.
  - Reduce Motion support.
  - Clear keyboard focus on the web version.
- **Posting anonymously is on by default** in Community, and you can change it each time.

### A warmer look (design refresh)

- **Headings in a rounder font** (Figtree), with body text still in Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- **New icons** with soft, rounded lines, including organs for the cancers that have one and five faces for how you feel.
- **A floating tab bar** that names every tab.
- **Today** shows how many steps a self-check has and sets the tip apart in warm yellow.
- **Learn** marks each self-check as due or done, and shows cancer types as a grid.
- **Journey** draws your week as one ribbon thread, with a knot for each day you checked in, and shows how your energy and pain compare with the week before.
- **Help** puts the crisis line on a bright teal card you can't miss.

### What we removed, and why

- **The news feed and stories:** the articles weren't checked by us, and the app should stay on awareness, early detection and support.
- **Wellness cards and reminders for hydration, movement and sun protection:** they weren't about cancer, and too many reminders get tuned out.
- **The notification center:** reminders open the right screen directly.
- **Saved articles:** articles are gone with the news feed. Saved questions, resources and now guides are in Profile → Saved.

### Finding things

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Home | Today |
| Info and Prevention | Learn |
| Forum | Community |
| Resources | Help |
| Profile → Notifications | Profile → Settings → Reminders |
| Saved questions and Saved resources | Profile → Saved (one screen) |

Your account, check-ins, treatments, questions and saved items carry over unchanged.
